## Research practice

- Begin focused methods reading and acquisition calibration, then perform a
  bounded comparison that distinguishes expected from changed future use.
  Prefer interpretable experimental contact over an expansive new framework.
- Name the mutable state, write operation, persistence/reset boundary, training
  objective and reader access. Distinguish information never usefully acquired,
  unavailable stored information and a reader unable to recover it. A failed
  reader or probe does not alone establish information loss.
- Keep future goals withheld at the relevant write time. Disclose distributional
  knowledge, teacher access and any retained history. Query-known conditions
  have hindsight information; privileged probes diagnose mechanisms rather
  than establish ordinary task performance. Charge storage, writing, reading
  and teacher computation under explicit comparison conditions.
- A negative result alone is not a stop condition. If the treatment cannot
  acquire its development cases, pursue bounded diagnosis or establish a
  functioning regime. Finite gradients and verified writes establish mechanics,
  not acquisition. Stop on explanatory progress, a demonstrated limitation or
  a concrete resource constraint; neither a neural win nor indefinite tuning
  is required. Preserve failed attempts and test developed claims on fresh
  evaluation material.
- Inspect closest public methods and implementations before claiming novelty.
  Record exact paper versions, reading scope, code revisions and configurations;
  distinguish author-reported results, new analysis and local reproduction.
  For arXiv discovery and metadata use `https://export.arxiv.org/api/query`.
  Cache responses with a descriptive User-Agent, one connection and at least
  three seconds between API/OAI requests across clients under your control;
  honor `Retry-After`. The [root source guidance](../../construct-2/AGENTS.md#research-sources)
  supplies access details.
- Keep supporting evidence and reproduction instructions here, with a concise
  `FINDINGS.md` publication linked from the README. Use version control to make
  revisions identifiable. Preserve evidence behind claims when excluding large
  model files or caches. Keep documentation proportional to the investigation.

  `https://mac-studio-7hr7.taile71f88.ts.net/engines/v1/chat/completions`

Serving access does not establish gradient or mutable-state access. Native MLX
training on the Mac Studio was demonstrated by
[procedure-transfer](../procedure-transfer/README.md); verify capabilities for
this study's chosen implementation. The
[S3 CPU implementation](../neural-memory-depth/README.md) is another bounded
lead. Preparation did not check endpoint availability or reproduce either route.

Choose and record a bounded resource envelope locally. Coordinate heavyweight
shared-machine runs with other investigators so training and measurement do
not interfere; do not build a scheduler. Independent reading, CPU work and
analysis need not wait on shared training capacity.
