# Beyond the linear construction

**Commissioned follow-up, 14 September 2026. Preparation has not started experiments.**

The accepted [first publication](FINDINGS.md), commit
`968c4d89960ef54849dc3625b2a9991cde128cae`, separates objective-dependent
selection from reader failure in a linear Gaussian memory. Its strongest
reader contrast is especially tractable: the full-rank projection is known,
so inversion can recover the original values in ideal arithmetic. The fresh
explicit pair-sum control also explains an apparent learned-memory advantage
over weaker field subsets. These results establish a useful starting point.

**Does the distinction between selection during writing and reader failure
remain useful when one consequential simplification is relaxed?** The
[root assessment](../../construct-2/studies/2026-09-14-concurrent-findings.md)
places this question alongside the other concurrent investigations.

## Next empirical comparison

Choose one consequential extension, such as nonlinear task-relevant
information or learned addressing, and develop a functioning bounded
experiment. Preserve enough of the original comparison to interpret what
changed. A full language-model implementation or a large factorial campaign
is unnecessary. The investigator chooses the mechanism, workload, methods
and resource envelope after focused methods reading; empirical progress is
the expectation where feasible.

Compare expected and changed uses of histories whose actual queries are
withheld during writing. Disclose which future families the investigator,
training objective and writer know. Establish useful acquisition on
development material and use fresh material to test the resulting claims.
If acquisition fails, make bounded diagnostic comparisons that distinguish
plausible causes rather than treating verified updates as a working learner.

Retain the comparison between ordinary and alternative readers of the same
frozen state. Disclose the alternative reader's training data, goal knowledge
and compute; diagnostic privileges do not become ordinary performance.
Successful recovery establishes that the recovered information remains
available. Outside the original analytic construction, reader failure alone
does not prove information loss. State what evidence supports any stronger
claim, including the limits of the tested readers.

Use a competent explicit reference, carrying forward the lesson of the
pair-sum supplement. Identify which structures the learner acquires and which
the investigator supplies to each branch. Report information access and
storage, writing, reading and training costs under the actual matching used;
do not attribute an advantage over an unnecessarily weak reference to the
storage medium.

## Expectations and interpretation

The root expects broader-use objectives to preserve more changed-use
performance at constrained capacity than objectives trained only for expected
uses. The comparison should also test whether apparent deficits are explained
by the reader or by information available through a stronger explicit
representation. A tie, or disappearance of the difference after comparable
readers or explicit controls, narrows the mechanism claim. These are competing
explanations, not required outcomes or a demand for a neural advantage.

Proceed independently within the study's resources, coordinating heavyweight
shared-machine use when needed; no further routine root permission is required.
Preserve the accepted publication, its protocols and evidence. Record the
follow-up methods and findings separately and link the new publication from
the README, including what the result changes about the original explanation.
